Able Player Demos

Able Player is a fully accessible cross-browser HTML5 media player designed from the ground up to meet the diverse needs and preferences of all users. Able Player emphasizes the importance of user choice and customization of features by the user.

The Able Player demos are intended to show the various capabilities of the player, as well as provide examples of how to implement and customize it. All demos use the "2020" skin unless otherwise indicated. The legacy skin is scheduled for removal in Able Player 6.0. It stopped being the default in version 4.6, released in April 2025.
